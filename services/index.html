Strategic HR Services for Purpose-Driven Teams

Work Nouveau helps mission-led organizations thrive with intention, offering people-first HR solutions that are strategic and deeply practical. From embedded partnerships to one-off projects, we bring clarity, structure, and momentum to your people operations.

Fractional Head of HR

Your strategic HR partner without the full-time price tag.

Think of us as your on-demand HR leader. For a fraction of the cost of a full time hire, we embed with your team, shaping strategy, solving challenges, providing you with senior-level insight. You get all the benefits of a seasoned HR leader, perfectly matched to your budget and needs.

Let’s Talk About Fractional Support

Project-Based HR Consulting

Targeted support for your most important people priorities.

Whether it’s crafting an employee handbook, navigating a tricky hire (or termination), or launching a team training, we jump in with care, clarity, and context. Our work is project based with a defined scope. No long term commitments, just focused solutions that move you forward.

Common projects include:

  • Employee handbook creation or updates
  • Hiring & termination guidance
  • Team engagement surveys
  • DEI or values-based policy development
  • Training & workshops for managers or staff

Leadership & Manager Development

Empowering your people to power your mission.

We equip your leaders and people managers with the tools, skills, and confidence to lead well, so their teams can thrive.  Through coaching, practical resources, and tailored learning experiences, we help them to sharpen their decision-making, strengthen their communication, and grow their teams with intention.

Options include:

  • Group sessions or cohort learning

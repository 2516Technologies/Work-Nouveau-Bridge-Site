About our Founder

Angela Smith is a sought-after people strategist, culture architect, and executive leader, dedicated to helping mission-driven organizations do good and do well. With deep experience in HR, organizational psychology, leadership development, and employee engagement, she partners with businesses to align purpose with performance, building workplaces where impact, engagement, and results thrive together.

A recognized thought leader, Angela delivers keynote speeches and workshops, inspiring leaders to create positive change while driving business success. She has been featured in Forbes, Mashable, and The Muse, and is an MBA graduate, PHR/SHRM-CP certified professional, and #IamRemarkable facilitator.
